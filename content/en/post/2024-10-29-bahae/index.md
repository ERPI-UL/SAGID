Bahaeddine Bekkouche joins the team, integrating into the ACTIBAC group on a two-year apprenticeship contract. Bahae, currently enrolled in the MIAGE program at the Institute of Digital Sciences, Management, and Cognition (IDMC), had already contributed to the chair's work during his end-of-degree internship from April to July earlier this year.

His work significantly advanced the development of the interface for the decision-support tool SAGID, which aims to assist managers in defining a sustainable maintenance policy for green dependencies. User testing was conducted, helping to more precisely determine managers' needs regarding the tool and its interface.

Bahae’s objective will be to continue developing this tool, making it more functional and gradually integrating research findings. The two-year contract will allow continuous and sustained progress on this task.
